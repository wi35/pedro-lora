The non Pandora box

Twenty-Four Stories Told by a Cockroach

The one telling you this story lives under your refrigerator.

Twenty-four stories told from the floor: from the crack in the wall, from the corner, from where nobody looks. The narrator is a cockroach. She survived the bomb. She dodges flyswatters. She falls in love, she dreams, she watches the evening news and has sharper things to say about world affairs than the people on screen.

Satire that bites

Pedro Lora writes satire that bites first and then, without warning, turns tender. Black humor, poetry that ambushes you in the middle of an infestation, and a street voice that refuses to be translated. Short stories you read in five minutes and carry for weeks: The Day of the Bomb, The Island in the Sky, The Terror of the Flyswatters, The Sky-Blue Cats, Pandora's Box Bursts Open.

About the author

Pedro Lora is a writer and illustrator. He writes satire that bites first and then, without warning, turns tender: black humor, poetry and absurd stories told from the places nobody looks. The non Pandora box, a collection of twenty-four stories narrated by a cockroach who lives under your refrigerator, is illustrated with his own drawings, one plate for every story. The book is also available in Spanish as La caja no pandora.
